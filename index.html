Haus of Sutra

Haus of Sutra is a queer led artist collective that produces nightlife events all around the Puget sound region. We host cabaret events that include burlesque shows, drag shows, comedy, live music and DJ dance nights.

Come join the community of people seeking fun, entertaining nightlife with a community vibe. We hope to meet you soon!
